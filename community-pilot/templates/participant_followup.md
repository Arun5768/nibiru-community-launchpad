# Participant follow-up template

Subject: Your next small Nibiru build step

Thank you for joining the Nibiru first-build session.

You do not need to finish a large project this week. Choose one useful next
step:

1. verify one Testnet 2 transaction and explain its receipt;
2. turn a blocker into a sanitized, reproducible support report;
3. improve one line in the workshop guide or translation; or
4. help another participant complete the same step safely.

Resources:

- Official Nibiru developer documentation: https://nibiru.fi/docs/dev
- Nibiru Debug Desk: https://nibiru-community-launchpad.arunchandel1780.workers.dev/
- Public community pilot materials: https://github.com/Arun5768/nibiru-community-launchpad/tree/main/community-pilot

If you share a repository, transaction hash, or improvement, please send only
public information. Never send a seed phrase, private key, or token.

I will publish an aggregate retrospective after seven days, including what did
not work. Tell me if you prefer not to be named or credited publicly.
