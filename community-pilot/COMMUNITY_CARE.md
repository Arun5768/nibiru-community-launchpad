# Community care and contributor recognition

This guide covers the first-build pilot’s moderation and recognition decisions.
It does not replace the rules of any official Nibiru channel.

## Community standard

The room should be safe for beginners to ask basic questions and precise enough
for experienced builders to give useful answers. Correct the information
without embarrassing the person. Ask for evidence without asking for secrets.

## Immediate moderation priorities

1. Remove seed phrases, private keys, authentication tokens, and exposed personal data from view as quickly as the platform allows.
2. Stop impersonation, fake support, malicious links, wallet-draining instructions, harassment, and investment solicitation.
3. Preserve a minimal private incident record only when follow-up is necessary.
4. Escalate security vulnerabilities through the project’s official security process, not a public workshop thread.

## Response pattern

When a technical question arrives:

1. **Acknowledge:** restate the problem in one line.
2. **Protect:** confirm that no secret is required.
3. **Narrow:** ask for the network, public hash or address, timestamp, exact error, and reproduction steps.
4. **Verify:** use official documentation and public chain evidence.
5. **Resolve or route:** answer what is known; label uncertainty; route product or protocol issues to the appropriate official channel.
6. **Close the loop:** record whether the guidance worked and improve the shared material when a pattern repeats.

## Recognition ladder

Recognition is based on helpful behavior, not message count.

| Level | Evidence | Recognition |
|---|---|---|
| First builder | Completes and explains one verifiable testnet action | Included in the session recap with consent |
| Helpful peer | Unblocks another participant safely and accurately | Public thank-you and invitation to co-answer follow-up questions |
| Contributor | Improves a guide, issue, example, translation, or repository | Linked contributor credit and review feedback |
| Community guide | Repeatedly helps others and documents recurring friction | Invitation to co-facilitate a future session |

No level implies employment, official Nibiru status, token rewards, or guaranteed
future opportunity.

## Contributor spotlight checklist

- Ask permission before publishing a name, image, quote, or wallet address.
- Link the actual helpful artifact or contribution.
- Explain the behavior being recognized.
- Avoid follower counts as the reason for selection.
- Rotate visibility so the same highly active people do not occupy every slot.
- Allow contributors to decline public recognition.

## Handling uncertain answers

Use: “I have not verified that yet. Here is the official source I will check,
and I’ll update this thread when I have evidence.” Do not convert confidence,
seniority, or repetition into a technical fact.

## Retrospective questions

- Which question appeared more than once?
- Where did people leave the flow?
- Which answer required an official maintainer?
- Did anyone share information they did not need to share?
- Who helped another participant, and was their contribution credited?
- What should be changed before the next session?
